FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before getting started.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. A simple video can take 2-4 weeks, while a full brand identity might take 6-8 weeks. We'll provide a clear timeline in your proposal.

What information do you need to provide a quote?

The more detail you can share, the more accurate our quote will be. We'll need to know your project goals, target audience, deliverables required, timeline, and budget range.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! We work with clients across the UK, Europe, North America, and Asia. We're comfortable collaborating remotely via video calls and project management tools.

What are your payment terms?

We typically require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon project completion. For larger projects, we can arrange milestone-based payments.

Can you work within our existing brand guidelines?

Yes, we're experienced at working with established brand guidelines. We can also help refresh or evolve your brand if needed.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

Client satisfaction is our priority. We include revision rounds in every project and work collaboratively to ensure the final output exceeds your expectations.
